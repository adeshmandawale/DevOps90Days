# Day 01 – My 90-Day DevOps Learning Plan

## Current Level
I am a working professional starting a focused journey to build practical DevOps and Cloud Engineering skills. I have a basic understanding of Linux, Git, Terraform, and other DevOps concepts, and I want to strengthen these fundamentals through hands-on practice.

## Why I Am Learning DevOps & Cloud
I want to become a confident DevOps engineer who can understand how applications move from development to production and can build, automate, deploy, monitor, and troubleshoot infrastructure and applications. My focus for these 90 days is practical learning rather than just completing theory or collecting certifications.

## My 3 Goals for the Next 90 Days
1. **Build strong DevOps fundamentals** – Become comfortable with Linux, networking, Git, cloud fundamentals, Docker, Terraform, CI/CD, and Kubernetes.
2. **Build and deploy a real project** – Create a practical application/infrastructure project and deploy it using AWS, containers, Infrastructure as Code, and CI/CD.
3. **Become job-ready for DevOps roles** – Improve troubleshooting, automation, documentation, and real-world problem-solving skills so I can confidently explain and demonstrate my work.

## 3 Core DevOps Skills I Will Build
- **Linux & Troubleshooting** – Processes, systemd, networking, permissions, logs, performance, and practical debugging.
- **CI/CD & Automation** – Git, GitHub, GitHub Actions, Docker, build/deployment pipelines, and automation.
- **Cloud & Infrastructure as Code** – AWS fundamentals, Terraform, infrastructure design, security basics, and deployment.

## Weekly Time Commitment
- **Weekdays:** 1.5–2 hours per day
- **Weekends:** 3–4 hours per day
- **Weekly target:** approximately **14–18 hours**

I will prioritize consistency over perfection. Even on busy days, I will spend some time learning, practicing, documenting, or reviewing what I learned.

## My 90-Day Commitment
I will learn in public, practice what I learn, document my progress in GitHub, and build something tangible throughout the journey. When I get stuck, I will troubleshoot systematically instead of skipping the problem.

**My goal is not simply to finish 90 days — it is to become significantly better at DevOps by the end of them.**
